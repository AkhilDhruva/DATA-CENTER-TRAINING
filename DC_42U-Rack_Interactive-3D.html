    How to use this trainer

    The 4-step dissection

    • 1 · Select component — click any device in the rack (or in the Rack Contents list).
    • 2 · Pull out — critical components slide out of the rack on their rails.
    • 3 · Open chassis — the casing splits apart and fades to a ghost.
    • 4 · Inspect internals — click any internal part in 3D, or use the Internals tab → Inspect.

    The ride

    • The trainer opens on a God's-eye view of a full data hall. Scroll down / swipe up / ↓ to zoom in, row by row, until one rack is isolated.
    • Start the ride plays it hands-free and carries on into the GPU node. Esc or Skip jumps straight to the rack.
    • Scroll back up past the full-rack view (or use Data hall) to fly back out to the hall.
    • Open the AI GPU node and choose Zoom into the silicon (or keep scrolling deeper) to dive from the GPU to the die, the L2 cache, a Tensor Core, the copper wiring, one transistor and finally the silicon atoms. Back to the rack rewinds.

    Controls

    • Drag to orbit · scroll down/pinch out to go deeper, scroll up to pull back · hover to identify.
    • Power view highlights the A/B feed path; Airflow view highlights blanking panels.
    • Esc closes overlays and exits dissection. Reset view returns to the full rack.

    Specs trace to EIA-310-D / ASHRAE TC9.9; vendor & deployment data reflects the 2026 US AI-infrastructure landscape.
